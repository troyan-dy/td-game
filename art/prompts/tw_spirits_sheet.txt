Use case: tower sprites for a dark-fantasy tower-defense game (pixel art). Image 1 shows the style; the turquoise ghost lantern towers in it show this tower's design.
Subject: "Ghost lantern": a dark iron and stone lantern shrine with glowing turquoise lantern glass on a square stone plinth with bones at its foot. Level 1: one lantern on a short stone post. Level 2: a taller post with a big central lantern and two small lanterns hanging from curved iron arms. Level 3: a tall gothic stone shrine with a pointed roof, a large central lantern, two hanging lanterns, small gargoyle skulls, faint turquoise runes.
Composition: one sheet with THREE separate sprites of the same tower in a row, left to right: level 1, level 2, level 3. Each tower is centred in its third of the canvas, stands on the same ground line near the bottom, with wide empty gaps between the towers. Level 1 fills about 55% of the canvas height, level 2 about 65%, level 3 about 78%. Each tower includes only its own small stone base, nothing else around it.
Camera: the same top-down 3/4 view from above at a slight angle as Image 1: the top of the base is visible and the front face of the tower.
Constraints: one tower design, three growth stages that clearly read as upgrades of the same object (same materials, same colours, more parts and ornaments per level). No ground, no grass, no shadow on the background, no flying ghosts or smoke clouds (the game draws those), no text, no numbers.
