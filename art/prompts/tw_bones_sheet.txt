Use case: tower sprites for a dark-fantasy tower-defense game (pixel art). Image 1 shows the style; the bone towers with a large bow of ribs in it show this design.
Subject: "Bone tower": a big crossbow-shaped frame made of pale bones and ribs, mounted on a round cracked grey-violet stone base, facing right. Level 1: small and simple, a few bones, one small skull. Level 2: bigger, more bones, a hanging purple banner, two skulls. Level 3: largest: dark-stone base with faint violet runes, a double frame of bones, a horned skull at the front, a small turquoise flame on top.
Composition: one sheet with three separate towers in a row, left to right: level 1, level 2, level 3, each centred in its third, on the same ground line, wide empty gaps between them. Level 1 is about half the canvas height, level 3 about three quarters.
Camera: the same top-down 3/4 view as Image 1.
Constraints: no ground, no shadow on the background, no text.
